# Showroom demonstration

The visual application preserves the Taarzan demonstration as the project's public-facing capability showcase. It is not itself a complete automotive curriculum or simulator.

## Controls

- Drag / touch to orbit; scroll / pinch to zoom.
- Select heritage or fictional 2026 concept.
- Select studio, midnight rain or architectural environment.
- Use the four camera presets and opt-in auto-rotation.
- Click hotspots for hood, intake pods, engine deck and canopy.
- Start ignition, hold Space or the pedal to rev, release to return toward idle.
- Mute, stop or select silent concept mode as appropriate.
- Open specifications or the source-reference story.

## Presentation boundaries

Violet paint, transmission glass, lighting and audio provide perceptual engagement. RPM, vibration and pitch are deliberately illustrative. A wheel or spoiler visible in the scene is not a validated mechanical assembly. The engine is recorded sound, not combustion synthesis. The number plate and design details are tribute elements.

## Teaching operation

Use a large display and confirm students can distinguish annotations. Begin with effects minimized, then enable them only when relevant to the question. Keep a static/text alternative ready. Explain design-brief figures and fictional targets before inviting engineering calculations.
