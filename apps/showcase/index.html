THE CAR. THE CINEMA. THE CULT FOLLOWING.

More than metal.
A memory in motion.

A violet silhouette. A spirit behind the wheel. This independent tribute revisits the car from the 2004 film Taarzan: The Wonder Car and imagines where that unmistakable attitude could go next.

From reference to real-time

This enclosed coupe is modeled procedurally from the repository’s photographs: a cab-forward glass canopy, sculpted side intakes, silver guide struts, and a rear bridge spoiler. It is an artistic reconstruction, not a scanned or factory CAD model.

Reference imagery, video, and original engine recording supplied in the project repository. An independent fan-made experience, not affiliated with DC Design or the film’s creators.
